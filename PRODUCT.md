# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS with Node.js (Express static server). No frameworks. Central JSON/JS data files for all mock data. Runs locally.

## Users

Regional Infrastructure Committee members, KSIA leadership, infrastructure coordination team, and sector leads. Senior decision-makers reviewing infrastructure readiness across a mega-airport city development. They need rapid situational awareness and actionable intelligence, not raw project data.

## Product Purpose

Provide the KSIA Regional Infrastructure Committee with an integrated decision-intelligence environment to assess infrastructure readiness across airport assets and milestones, identify gaps and dependencies, and support timely committee decisions and interventions. This is committee decision intelligence, not project reporting.

## Positioning

The only integrated view connecting airport assets, milestone dates, demand, supply, external-entity projects, interim/permanent solutions, funding, land, approvals, dependencies and required committee actions — across and beyond KSIA boundaries.

## Operating Context

Used in committee meetings and by leadership reviewing infrastructure status. Data is representative/sample for this POC. The primary demonstration storyline follows Terminal 6 potable-water readiness end-to-end. Screens are navigated from a central landing page. Global filters (Major Development, KSIA Area, Infrastructure Stream, External Entity, Time Horizon) persist across all screens.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 10 screens total: Landing Page, Executive Overview, 8 detailed modules
- All data from central configurable data files, never hard-coded in UI
- Readiness statuses: Ready, Conditional, At Risk, Blocked, Not Assessed
- Project statuses: On Track, At Risk, Delayed, Blocked, Completed, Not Started
- Currency: SAR (Saudi Riyal), large values in billions
- Time horizons: 2025–2050+
- Infrastructure streams: Mobility & Roads, Public Transport, Potable Water, Wastewater, Power & Energy, District Cooling, Digital & Telecom, Stormwater & Drainage, Waste & Environmental, Natural Gas
- Includes AI chatbot assistant ("Ask KSIA Assistant")

## Brand Commitments

EY branding. Mixed light-and-dark visual theme: white/light-grey canvas, dark charcoal header and selected dark intelligence panels, EY yellow (#FFE600) for emphasis only. Crisp high-contrast typography. The approved KSIA airport-city aerial image serves as the primary spatial reference.

## Product Principles

1. Decision intelligence over project reporting — surface what the Committee needs to act on, not raw status
2. Integrated view — connect assets, supply, demand, funding, prerequisites, dependencies and scenarios in one environment
3. Data-driven readiness — readiness is calculated from capacity, timing, prerequisites and interfaces, not subjective assessment
4. Configurable sample data — all values from central data files, enabling rapid storyline changes

## Accessibility & Inclusion

High-contrast dark/light panels. Status communicated through color AND text labels. Large readable typography for committee-room projection.
