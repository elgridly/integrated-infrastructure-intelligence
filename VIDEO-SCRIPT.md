## Prologue — Setting the Stage

**1. The Scale**
> *"Across the Middle East, the most ambitious developments in history are taking shape — new cities, airport cities, giga-projects, and economic zones that will redefine how people live, work, and move."*

**2. The Invisible Risk**
> *"The greatest threat to delivering them isn't construction. It's coordination. Multiple entities delivering interdependent infrastructure on separate timelines — where one delay silently cascades into a development that can't open on time."*

**3. The Blind Spot**
> *"Today, infrastructure readiness is fragmented — scattered across disconnected systems and siloed dashboards. By the time a gap surfaces, the window to act has already closed."*

**4. The Question**
> *"What if leadership could see every asset, every dependency, every gap, and every decision required — in real time — as a living intelligence system that connects cause to consequence?"*

**5. The Answer**
> *"This is why we built I-Cubed. A platform that doesn't just track infrastructure — it connects it. From demand to supply. From gap to solution. From risk signal to action. So leadership acts before the chain breaks."*

## Production Notes

### Pacing
- **Scenes 1-2:** 25 seconds — establish the platform and AI capability
- **Scenes 3-4:** 30 seconds — spatial intelligence and filtering power
- **Scene 5:** 20 seconds — project drill-down
- **Scenes 6-8:** 35 seconds — supply-demand narrative (the hero story)
- **Scene 9:** 15 seconds — closing
- **Total runtime:** ~2 minutes 5 seconds

### Tone
Confident, authoritative, forward-looking. This is not a software demo — it is the unveiling of a new category of infrastructure intelligence. The narration should feel like a keynote reveal, not a training walkthrough.

### Music
Cinematic, building tension through scenes 6-8 (the gap story), resolving in scene 9. Think architectural documentary meets product launch.

### Key Phrases to Emphasise
- *"One click. The entire platform responds."*
- *"The gap isn't a forecast — it's a fact."*
- *"Every decision has a deadline. Every deadline has a consequence."*
- *"Enabling today. Ready for tomorrow."*
